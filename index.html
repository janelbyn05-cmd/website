About

Design and code, one person, fewer handoffs.

I started in print layout, moved into web development in 2018, and have spent the years since shipping interfaces people use all day: booking tools, dispatch boards, point-of-sale screens.

I work in the browser from the first sketch. Prototypes are real HTML, so you test with actual typing, scrolling, and slow connections before anyone writes a spec.

Outside of client work I teach a monthly accessibility workshop for local student groups and keep a small library of type specimens I've photographed around Metro Manila.
